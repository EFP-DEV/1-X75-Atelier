Classification reminder

Each book must have one mandatory category, one mandatory theme, and optional tags.

Category
The required type of book, such as Opening theory, Tactics, or Endgame.
Theme
The required subject world of the book, such as Bananas, Canopy, or Territory.
Tags
Optional retrieval labels, such as beginner-friendly, classic, or fast games.
